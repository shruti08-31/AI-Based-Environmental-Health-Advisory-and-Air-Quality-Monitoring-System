# Ground-Level Ozone (O3) & Photochemical Formation
Source: WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines & US EPA Ground-Level Ozone Reference

Tropospheric (ground-level) ozone (O3) is a secondary pollutant formed when nitrogen oxides (NOx) and volatile organic compounds (VOCs) react in the presence of sunlight. Consequently, O3 levels frequently peak during sunny afternoon hours and warm stagnant weather. Ozone is a strong oxidant that causes airway irritation, coughing, chest tightness, and temporary decreases in lung capacity during outdoor exercise.
