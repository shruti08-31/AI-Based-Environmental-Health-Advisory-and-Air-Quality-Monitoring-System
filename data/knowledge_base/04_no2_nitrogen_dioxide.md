# Nitrogen Dioxide (NO2) & Combustion Emissions
Source: WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines & US EPA NO2 Reference

Nitrogen dioxide (NO2) is a reddish-brown reactive gas formed primarily during high-temperature fossil fuel combustion in motor vehicles, power plants, and industrial engines. Concentrations are typically elevated near busy traffic corridors and urban intersections. NO2 inflames the lining of the airways, increases bronchial reactivity to allergens, and contributes to the atmospheric formation of ground-level ozone (O3) and secondary nitrate aerosols.
