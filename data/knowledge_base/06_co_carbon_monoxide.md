# Carbon Monoxide (CO) & Incomplete Combustion
Source: WHO Air Quality Guidelines & US EPA Carbon Monoxide Reference

Carbon monoxide (CO) is an odorless, colorless gas generated by incomplete combustion of carbon-based fuels in petrol/diesel vehicles, generators, stoves, and biomass burning. When inhaled, CO binds to hemoglobin with much higher affinity than oxygen to form carboxyhemoglobin, reducing oxygen delivery to vital organs such as the heart and brain. Individuals with coronary artery disease are particularly vulnerable to chest pain (angina) during elevated CO exposure.
