# Health Effects & Vulnerable / Sensitive Population Groups
Source: WHO & Public-Health Respiratory Protection Guidance

Air pollution affects both healthy individuals during intense exposure and vulnerable groups at lower thresholds. Sensitive populations include people with asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), cardiovascular disease, children (whose lungs are still developing and who breathe more air per unit body weight), older adults, and pregnant individuals. Public-health advisories provide preventive risk reduction guidance and never replace clinical medical diagnosis.
