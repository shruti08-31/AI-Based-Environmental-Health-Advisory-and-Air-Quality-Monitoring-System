# Sulfur Dioxide (SO2) & Industrial Fuel Burning
Source: WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines & US EPA SO2 Reference

Sulfur dioxide (SO2) is a colorless gas produced primarily by burning sulfur-containing fossil fuels such as coal and heavy fuel oil in power generation, industrial boilers, and metal smelting. SO2 rapidly irritates the nose, throat, and airways, causing bronchoconstriction even after brief exposures—particularly in people with asthma during physical exertion. In the atmosphere, SO2 oxidizes into sulfate aerosols that contribute to PM2.5 mass.
