# Coarse Particulate Matter (PM10) & Dust Resuspension
Source: WHO Global Air Quality Guidelines & CPCB Particulate Reference

PM10 encompasses inhalable particles with diameters of 10 micrometers or smaller, including both fine particles and coarser thoracic fractions (2.5 to 10 micrometers). Coarse PM10 is commonly associated with mechanical crushing, construction and demolition activities, unpaved roads, windblown soil dust, and resuspended road dust. Elevated PM10 irritates the eyes, nose, and upper respiratory tract and aggravates chronic bronchitis and asthma.
